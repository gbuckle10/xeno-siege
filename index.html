Your maps
Goal: aliens pour out of the green rifts and march down the lanes. Each one that reaches your base drains shields. Lose all 100 and it's over.
  • Build: pick a tower, then tap an empty pad beside a lane.
  • Upgrade: tap a tower. Level 2, then choose one of two specializations, then Mk II. Towers go offline for a few seconds while upgrading.
  • Waves: press Launch to start. After that a timer brings each next wave automatically. Call it early for a bonus.
  • Know your enemy: Wasps fly straight at your base (Mortars and Cryo can't hit them). Wardens shield the aliens around them. Armor grows as waves go on.
  • Camera: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Sunlance: tap it, then tap the map to fire a beam across the whole map in that direction.
  • Powers: kills charge your Energy. Spend it on meteor showers, orbital strikes and more (keys Z X C V B).
  • Your maps: build your own map and wave rules with the editor, and share them with a code.
  • Tip: Shellbacks are armored. Railguns, Arc Coils, Ion Lances and the Sunlance ignore armor.
